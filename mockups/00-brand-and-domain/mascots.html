Kids zone mascot sketches

Quick sketches to compare personality, not final art. Final artwork would be drawn properly after you pick a direction.

Cori (Littlecore)

A hard-hatted reactor dome with a glowing core on their belly. Curious, a bit clumsy, always building something. Says "Little core, big zap!"

Kilo (Kilocore)

A small robot with a core ring on the chest and an amber antenna. Likes counting and measuring. Says "Let's measure it!"

Amp & Atom

Two friends: Amp the lightning bolt is fast and excitable, Atom is round, calm and thoughtful. They disagree, then work it out together.
