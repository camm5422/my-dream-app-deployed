Kilocore

From the socket to the core.

Idea: "kilo" signals units and measurement (kilowatt, kilovolt) and "core" points at the reactor. The logo is reactor core rings crossed by a grid line, so it says both halves of the subject. Clean and technical, with the Kids zone kept bright. Mascot: Kilo, a small robot with a core on its chest.
