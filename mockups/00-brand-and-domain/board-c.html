Amp & Atom

Two curious friends. One powered-up world.

Idea: electricity is Amp (a lightning bolt) and the reactor is Atom (a round, orbiting character). Two friends give you natural story pairs for lessons, quizzes and classroom cards, and the name has no L/R or V/B sounds, so it is the easiest to say for non-native English speakers. Bold and colourful, with the Pros zone in a dark, calm version.
