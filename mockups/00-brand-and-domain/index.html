Other things to know

  • Fonts: these mockups load Google Fonts. Google Fonts can be blocked or slow in mainland China, so the real site would host the fonts itself.
  • Colours: button and text colours were chosen for readable contrast on phones, but they have not been tested with a contrast tool yet.
  • The three zones look different on purpose: Kids is bright and rounded, Students is game-like, Pros is dark and control-room style. Each board shows all three.
