# Littlecore brand and voice guide

## The brand in brief
- **Name:** Littlecore (one word, capital L, lower-case "core"). A small modular reactor is a "little core".
- **Tagline:** Big power from a little core.
- **What it is:** a free, phone-first learning site about electricity, grids and small modular reactors, from a senior electrical engineer working in SMRs. Paid sessions, school bookings and kits are the shop behind the free learning.
- **Personality:** curious, warm, honest, never talking down. Serious about safety and accuracy, light about everything else.
- **Mascot:** Cori, a hard-hatted reactor dome with a glowing core. Used in the Kids zone and as a friendly guide elsewhere (sparingly in the Pros zone).

## Visual identity
Tokens live in `demo/src/styles/tokens.css`; fonts in `demo/src/styles/fonts.css`; logo files in `demo/public/brand/`.
- **Logo:** the reactor-dome mark (`logo-mark.svg`) with the "Littlecore" wordmark set in Baloo 2 Bold: "Little" in ink, "core" in deep teal. Give the mark clear space of at least a quarter of its width.
- **Colours:** sunshine `#FFC531`, teal `#12A4A0` (logo) and deep teal `#0B7673` (buttons, links), ink `#1D2B4F`, cream `#FFF7E0`, coral `#FF6B5B` (decoration) and deep coral `#C93A2D` (Kids buttons), night teal `#10222B` (Pros).
- **Fonts:** Baloo 2 for headings, Nunito for body text, IBM Plex Mono for data and readouts.
- **Accessibility:** text and buttons meet WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1). Do not put teal `#12A4A0` or coral `#FF6B5B` behind white text.
- **Buttons:** at least 48px tall, one clear main action per screen.

## Voice, all zones
- Plain words, short sentences. Explain a term the first time it appears.
- Write for readers who may not speak English as a first language: avoid idioms and slang, one idea per sentence.
- Use real units (V, Hz, W) and say them out loud ("fifty hertz").
- Be honest about uncertainty. Say "simplified" or "illustrative" when it is.
- Never vendor-specific, employer-confidential or export-controlled detail. Public sources only.
- Every lesson and simulator carries: "Educational, not for design."
- Use they/them for anyone whose pronouns are not stated.

## Voice by zone
| Zone | Age | Tone | Sounds like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kids | 8-11 | Playful, excited, cause and effect, Cori talks to you | "Whoa, that is a lot of zap! Let's find out where it comes from." |
| Students | 12-15 | Game-like, challenges, real units, light humour | "Your mission: keep the city lit. Careful, demand just went up." |
| Pros | 16+ and early career | Calm, precise, control-room style, sources cited | "Frequency holds at 50 Hz while supply matches demand. Here is how." |

## Words to use and avoid
- Use: "small modular reactor (SMR)", "power station", "grid", "the little core".
- Avoid: fear words ("meltdown", "deadly") unless explaining a safety concept accurately; claims like "safe", "clean" or "cheap" without a source; "simple" for things that are not.
- Safety lessons are matter-of-fact: what protects people, and why.
